Decline due to Location is Far · CSP Assist & Capture — §7 rewrite

Acceptance criteria: before & after

The 11 acceptance criteria from the signed-off PRD, rewritten against two independent review passes. Each old AC is shown beside its replacement, with what changed and why it's better. Format is unchanged — same §7 shape, same ID system; bundled ACs are split into atomic ones, and every outcome is bound to an observable domain fact, never an implementation detail.

Scope: §7 only — an evaluation, not a PRD change Status: proposal for a v1.1 amendment Not applied: the PRD MD/HTML are untouched ↓ Second pass: strict QA of the rewrites

The two axes, and the one rule

Every AC was scored on two things only, then rewritten to pass both.

Correct

Faithful to the PRD's own rules and internally consistent — the When must match the canon (§3a / §3b), and the Given must not presuppose the outcome.

Testable

Two people who've never met mark it pass/fail identically, using domain nouns only — no "recorded", "deliberate", "as before".

  • One AC = one independently-breakable claim. A partial pass must be reportable, so bundles split along failure domains.
  • Every Then is an observable domain fact — a state of an entity you already own (booking, allocation, signal, points, timer, standing). No schema, no tables.
  • Define by consequence, not label. "Deliberate" isn't a flag — it's "carries the shown points and emits a signal".
  • Unknowns are marked, not guessed. [CLARIFY] flags a ruling you owe, not a defect invented by the writer.

Before → after, one at a time

Left = the signed-off AC. Right = the replacement(s). Below each, the specific moves and why they make it verifiable.

AC-CONF-1Confirm "I won't be able to connect" (T1)1 → 3
Before
Given a CSP shown 3 of his active connections, When he taps "I won't be able to connect", Then the decline is recorded as deliberate, the booking is removed + confirmation (S4), and a signal is pushed carrying the ids + 3 points in order.
After
CONF-1a — Then the booking is removed from him and a decline for this (booking, CSP) is registered carrying the 3 points he was shown, in order, as the points he rejects.
CONF-1b — Then he sees the S4 confirmation showing the booking was removed and his reason.
CONF-1c — Then a signal is emitted to Genie for this booking carrying {connection id, customer id, CSP, capture point = decline, reason, the 3 points in order}.
Why it's better
  • "Deliberate" is gone — replaced by its two observable consequences (carries the shown points + emits a signal), the exact things that distinguish T1 from T3. No hidden flag to guess at.
  • Split by system — allocation record (1a), CSP-facing UI (1b), outbound emission (1c). A missing S4 no longer fails the emission check.
  • Every Then is a domain fact; no table or schema named.
AC-CONF-2Only shown points are sent (G1 invariant)tightened
Before
Given the same confirm, When the signal is emitted, Then it names only the points that were shown — no point the CSP was not shown ever appears.
After
CONF-2 — Given the intercept showed 3 points while Genie's response contained 5, When he confirms, Then the signal carries exactly those 3 shown points and none of the 2 that were not shown.
Why it's better
  • The test can now fail. Old Given (shown = all Genie returned) can't distinguish a correct signal from a leaky one. Forcing shown ⊂ returned makes the zero-tolerance G1 invariant actually provable.
  • Already the best-formed AC — this only sharpens the precondition; nothing else changes.
AC-BACK-1Back-off "OK, I'll connect" (T2)1 → 4 (+1)
Before
Given a CSP shown his nearby connections, When he taps "OK, I'll connect", Then no decline is recorded and no signal is sent, the booking stays with him, and the back-off event is captured for measurement.
After shared Given: booking pre-acceptance · Genie returns 3 · intercept showing them · decline not yet sent [Q1]
BACK-1a — Then the booking remains assigned to the same CSP, and nothing that would exclude him, cool him down, or count against his standing has been applied.
BACK-1b — Then no signal is emitted to Genie [Q2: and, if in scope, no decline-pattern to Quality OS / Enforcement OS].
BACK-1c — Then the booking is present and actionable in his feed, and the acceptance window is [Q3: unchanged / paused].
BACK-1d — Then the system can answer that the intercept was shown and he reconsidered — exactly one such outcome, correlatable to the intercept-shown moment, carrying the capture point and the count of points shown (3).
BACK-2 — back-off at the install-failure capture point (R5 parity) [Q5: post-state].
Why it's better
  • Four failure domains, four rows — allocation, emission, feed+timer, measurement. A leaked Genie signal (your G1 invariant) is now reported separately from a missing analytics event.
  • "No decline recorded" → domain states — assignment, exclusion, cooldown, standing. The old phrase let a tester pass while a decline-pattern had already reached Enforcement OS.
  • "Stays with him" → a definite state + the timer decision (a product choice, not implementation) — which protects MQ-7's timeout metric.
  • Honest status — 1a & 1d shippable; 1b, 1c, BACK-2 are OPEN on your rulings, instead of the old row being falsely "Settled".
AC-STR-1No points / skip (T3)correctness fix
Before
Given Genie returns no nearby points, When he confirms the decline, Then the decline is recorded, no signal is sent, and he is told the check was skipped.
After
STR-1 — When the decline is submitted and no points return within the wait, Then the decline is registered for this (booking, CSP), no signal is emitted, and he sees a neutral "check skipped" note — never blocked or left waiting.
Why it's better
  • The When now matches the canon. In T3 there is no confirm step — §3a routes straight from "no points" to a finalised decline. "Confirms" described a screen that doesn't exist; "submitted" is what T3 actually says.
  • Outcomes are domain facts (registered / no signal / neutral note), and it names the shared T3 outcome it holds with FAIL-1 so the two can't drift.
AC-WF-1Parity across both capture pointsreframed
Before
Then both emit a signal, and the only differences recorded are the capture point and the outcome copy.
After
WF-1 — Then both signals carry the same shown points, in the same order, as rejected; the decline's capture point = decline and the install-failure's = install-failure.
Why it's better
  • No universal diff. "Only differences are…" forces a tester to compare two whole records against a schema that doesn't exist. The rewrite asserts the specific equalities (same points, same order) and the one intended difference — both directly observable.
AC-WF-2Install-failure confirm outcomebound to observable
Before
Then the failure is recorded and the task is handed back for re-routing (not shown as "removed").
After
WF-2 — Then the task stays accepted, no failure strike is recorded against him, the booking re-enters routing (available for re-assignment) rather than removed from him, and the signal's capture point = install-failure. [Q5]
Why it's better
  • "Handed back" → a routing-level observable (re-enters routing / available for re-assignment) that reads differently from the pre-acceptance "removed", so a tester can tell the two paths apart.
AC-GRD-1Capture-only guardrail (G4)reframed
Before
When the signal reaches Genie, Then this feature has taken no routing or de-listing action itself.
After
GRD-1 — When the decline is confirmed, Then the feature's only outbound action is the single Genie signal — it issues no routing, assignment, or de-listing call.
Why it's better
  • Real trigger. "Signal reaches Genie" is outside this feature's control; "the decline is confirmed" is an action it owns.
  • Bounded negative. "No action anywhere in the system" is untestable; "the feature's only outbound action is the one signal" is observable at the feature's own boundary.
AC-FAIL-1Genie timeout (never block)bound to a number
Before
When Genie's API has not returned points in time, Then the app lets him complete the decline — never left spinning — and no signal is sent.
After
FAIL-1 — When Genie's points do not arrive within the wait W [Q6: Eng-supplied value], Then the decline completes without the intercept — never blocked or waiting beyond W — and no signal is emitted.
Why it's better
  • "Spinning" → "waiting beyond W". Correct as written, but not runnable without a number. Referencing the Eng-owned value W keeps §5's "no config" decision intact while making the boundary executable at test time.
AC-REG-1Regression — other reasonstightened
Before
Given this feature ships, When a CSP declines for another reason, Then no intercept, no fetch, no signal — and routing / DAS behave exactly as before.
After
REG-1 — Given a CSP declines for a reason other than Location is Far, When he submits it, Then no intercept appears, no nearby-points fetch is made, and no signal is emitted — Part 1 capture is the only behaviour.
Why it's better
  • Real Given/When — a state and an action, not "when it ships".
  • Keeps the three crisp negatives (the actually-testable part) and drops "behave exactly as before", which is a whole regression suite masquerading as one Then.
AC-RACE-1Late points after fallbackbound to a number
Before
Given the app already proceeded (T3), When Genie's points arrive afterwards, Then they are ignored and the decline stands.
After
RACE-1 — Given the wait W elapsed and the decline is registered (T3), When points arrive after W, Then no intercept appears and the registered decline is unchanged. [Q6: W]
Why it's better
  • "Afterwards" is anchored to W — the same value FAIL-1 uses. Two clean observables (no intercept, decline unchanged). Well-shaped already; this only pins the threshold.
AC-DUP-1Double-tap idempotencyGiven/When un-inverted
Before
Given a decline already recorded and emitted, When the CSP double-taps, Then exactly one decline and one signal exist.
After
DUP-1 — Given the intercept is shown with points, When the CSP double-taps "I won't be able to connect", Then exactly one decline is registered and one signal emitted for this booking, and the second tap adds nothing (S4 shown once).
Why it's better
  • Cause and effect restored. The old Given presupposed the outcome ("already recorded") — so the double-tap had nothing to prove. Now the double-tap is the cause and the single record is what must hold.
  • Says what the second tap does (nothing; S4 once) — the old one was silent on it.

What the rewrite is waiting on

Every [CLARIFY] above maps to one of these. They are product rulings, not writing problems — and most block a real build decision, not just an AC.

  1. Q1 — "Submit" = the button tap (client-side, before the decline call)? If it means the API call, "no decline recorded" is unbuildable — the decline has already fired and re-routed. highest risk
  2. Q2 — Are Quality OS / Enforcement OS decline signals in scope for "nothing held against me"? Both receive decline data today; neither is named in the PRD.
  3. Q3 — The acceptance clock while the modal is open: unchanged, paused, or restarted? Decides whether MQ-7's timeout metric stays clean.
  4. Q4 — Is "deliberate" a stored attribute or narrative-only? If narrative, CONF-1 asserts it purely through its consequences (points + signal).
  5. Q5 — Install-failure back-off / confirm post-state? "Stays with him" and "handed back" mean something different post-acceptance.
  6. Q6 — The Genie wait value W (Eng-owned). FAIL-1 and RACE-1 need a number at test time even though §5 keeps it out of the PRD.
  7. Q7 — The reason value literal is owned by Part 1 (Reason Set). Named as an input, not embedded here — consistent with "no enums in the PRD".
Reject-with-reason (not adopted): the reviewers wanted the reason enum and the timeout number written into this PRD. Both are deliberate deferrals — resolved by naming the owner (Part 1 · Eng), not by embedding implementation. That keeps your what/why stance intact.
Second pass · adversarial QA of the rewrites

Do the new ACs actually hold up?

The same strict rubric — Testable · Unambiguous · Atomic · Measurable · Solution-agnostic · Complete (1–5) — turned back on the rewritten set. Verdict up front: the rewrite closed the big holes (correctness, the worst observability offenders), but a strict re-read finds a next layer — a few rows still bundle 3–4 claims, I introduced some new soft terms, and two blockers remain unresolved (the signal's observable form; the timeout number). Being honest about this is the point: one pass is never enough.

4 of 16 are Ready (CONF-1b, CONF-2, BACK-1d, DUP-1). The rest need a second tightening, and BACK-2 is still a stub. That's a real improvement over the original set (where the best-scored row, AC-CONF-2, was the only shippable one) — but not "done".

B. Specific defects (quoted)

Residual bundle
STR-1 · "the decline is registered … no signal is emitted … a neutral note … never blocked or left waiting"

Four claims in one row — the exact thing I split BACK-1 to avoid. Same in FAIL-1 (completes + never-blocked + no-signal) and WF-2 (accepted + no strike + re-enters routing + capture point). These must split like BACK-1 did.

New vague term I introduced
BACK-1a · "nothing that would … count against his standing"

"Standing" is undefined — a tester can't enumerate what counts. I traded "no decline recorded" for a cleaner phrase but left one soft word. Fix by naming the exact set (no decline-exit, no cooldown, no assignment-count change) — [code-verify the set — this is the reviewer's D1 list].

New vague term
BACK-1c · "present and actionable in his feed"

"Actionable" reads two ways. Say what he can do: "still in his feed and he can still accept it."

New vague term
WF-2 · "no failure strike is recorded against him"

"Strike" isn't a defined entity. Replace with the domain fact: "no decline-exit or cooldown is applied to him."

Still a boundary, not an AC
GRD-1 · "the feature's only outbound action is the single Genie signal"

Better than "no action anywhere", but "only outbound action" is still a universal negative over the feature's whole surface. Assert the specific negatives instead: "issues no re-route, no re-assignment, and no de-listing call."

Unresolved blocker — carried, not fixed
CONF-1c · CONF-2 · BACK-1b · "a signal is emitted to Genie carrying {…}"

I named the fields, but not the observable handle — where a tester inspects the emitted signal. This is the reviewers' 9-of-11 blocker, and the rewrite doesn't close it; it just makes it visible. Resolution is a citation, not a redesign: verified at Genie's receive boundary, per the signed-off Genie PRD.

Missing number — carried
STR-1 · FAIL-1 · RACE-1 · "within the wait" / "beyond W" / "after W"

W is referenced but has no value (Q6). Correct shape, unrunnable until Eng supplies the number at test time. Not a defect in the PRD (§5 keeps it out by design) — a defect in calling these ACs "executable" today.

Mild bundle
CONF-1a · "the booking is removed from him and a decline … is registered"

Two facts (UI removal + the decline record). Split cleanly — removal is CONF-1b's territory, the record is its own.

C. Open questions — ranked by risk

  1. The Genie signal's observable form. Where does a tester inspect the emitted signal — the Genie receive boundary, an event log? Blocks 9 of the 16. highest new
  2. The exact "adverse-consequence" set for a back-off / decline (BACK-1a). Enumerate what must NOT be applied — decline-exit, cooldown, assignment-count, decline-pattern to Quality/Enforcement — and code-verify it (D1).
  3. Q1 — "submit" = the button tap (pre-call)? Still the story-level risk.
  4. Q6 — the wait value W for STR-1 / FAIL-1 / RACE-1.
  5. Q2 / Q3 / Q5 — downstream scope, acceptance-clock behaviour, install-failure post-state (unchanged from the first pass).
  6. Definitions of "standing" and "strike" — or drop both for enumerated domain facts.
